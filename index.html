CINERYN FILMS YouTube Agent

CINERYN FILMS YouTube Agent is a private creator workflow used by the channel owner to understand channel performance and carry out explicitly approved YouTube account actions.

What it does
  • Reads the authenticated YouTube channel identity and owned video metadata.
  • Reads private YouTube Analytics metrics for channel reporting and decision support.
  • When the owner explicitly authorizes it, can upload videos, update metadata, schedule videos, and use YouTube account-management permissions.

How Google data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the requested YouTube Agent functionality for the authenticated channel. OAuth credentials are not published in source control. The service does not sell Google user data.

Read the Privacy Policy for details about access, storage, sharing, retention, revocation, and deletion.
